## The review

> Booked Monday for breakfast. Waiter sat me at 7 a.m., served cold emails, then charged a full week for one grey morning.

22 words, 120 characters — inside the 25-word / 180-character gate, one line, plain text. It reads as a genuine diner's complaint: a booking, a waiter, a table at 7 a.m., a dish served cold (emails), and a bill that charges a full week for a single grey morning. The joke only works for Monday — no other day bills you for the whole week before breakfast.

## Run it

